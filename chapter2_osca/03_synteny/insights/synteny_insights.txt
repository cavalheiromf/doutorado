# Comparative Genomics and Synteny Analysis of Vellozia genomes and the OSCA Locus

Genome-wide comparative synteny analysis between Vellozia peripherica and Vellozia tubiflora revealed an exceptionally high degree of macro-collinearity across their nine chromosomes, demonstrating a conserved, one-to-one chromosome-level architectural relationship (e.g., vp_chr1A aligning directly to vt_chr1A) with no major inter-chromosomal translocations since their evolutionary divergence. A total of 297 syntenic blocks were identified globally, highlighting a remarkably stable genomic landscape.

At the micro-synteny level, focus on the calcium-permeable channel OSCA gene family identified a perfectly conserved syntenic block on chromosome 7A containing a tandem duplication cluster of OSCA1.3, OSCA1.4, and OSCA1.5 (Vper genes g186390, g186400, and g186410; Vtub genes g166590, g166600, and g166610, respectively). The local region is flanked by 24 highly syntenic orthologous gene pairs, confirming the robust structural preservation of this locus. This perfect micro-collinearity and physical contiguity of the OSCA1.3/1.4/1.5 cluster in both species indicate that this tandem expansion event predated the speciation of V. peripherica and V. tubiflora, and the cluster has been strictly maintained under strong purifying selection, potentially due to its crucial role in environmental sensing and stress adaptation in resurrection plants.

### New Key Findings on Intragenomic Duplications (Self-Synteny):

1. **Conserved Paleoduplication / Segmental Duplication (Chr 1A <-> Chr 5A)**:
   An ancient large-scale segmental duplication block connects Chromosome 1A and Chromosome 5A in both Vellozia genomes. This ancestral duplication event simultaneously expanded two distinct subfamilies of the OSCA family:
   * **In V. peripherica (Vper)**: 
     - VpOSCA2.2 (Chr 1A) <---> VpOSCA2.1 (Chr 5A)
     - VpOSCA3.1 (Chr 1A) <---> VpOSCA3.2 (Chr 5A)
   * **In V. tubiflora (Vtub)**: 
     - VtOSCA2.2 (Chr 1A) <---> VtOSCA2.1 (Chr 5A)
     - VtOSCA3.1 (Chr 1A) <---> VtOSCA3.2 (Chr 5A)
   This conserved inter-chromosomal collinear relationship confirms that the duplication of these OSCA subfamilies predates the speciation event of V. peripherica and V. tubiflora, likely tracing back to an ancestral whole-genome duplication (WGD) or a massive segmental duplication in the Velloziaceae lineage.

2. **Strictly Conserved OSCA1.3/1.4/1.5 Tandem Cluster (Chr 7A)**:
   The physical contiguity and tandem duplication of the OSCA1.3, OSCA1.4, and OSCA1.5 genes on Chromosome 7A are perfectly conserved with a gap of 0 intermediate genes in both V. peripherica and V. tubiflora:
   * Vper Cluster: VpOSCA1.3 (g186390) -> VpOSCA1.4 (g186400) -> VpOSCA1.5 (g186410)
   * Vtub Cluster: VtOSCA1.3 (g166590) -> VtOSCA1.4 (g166600) -> VtOSCA1.5 (g166610)
   This perfect preservation points to highly constrained purifying selection over this sensory locus, emphasizing its potential functional significance in these desiccation-tolerant species.

3. **V. peripherica-Specific Tandem Expansion of OSCA2.4 (Chr 1A)**:
   A clear evolutionary divergence was detected on Chromosome 1A. V. peripherica possesses an adjacent tandem duplication of the OSCA2.4 gene, resulting in two consecutive copies: VpOSCA2.4a (g023030) and VpOSCA2.4b (g023020) with a gap of 0 intermediate genes. In contrast, V. tubiflora retains only a single copy, VtOSCA2.4 (g020650). This represents a lineage-specific tandem duplication event in V. peripherica (or a gene loss event in V. tubiflora) since their divergence, indicating active localized reshaping of the environmental stress response machinery.

### 4. Comparative Evolutionary Selection Analysis (HyPhy RELAX & Contrast-FEL)

To correlate genomic synteny and gene duplication structures with evolutionary forces, comparative selection analyses were conducted across the 27 OSCA paralogs between the desiccation-tolerant *Vellozia tubiflora* (Test / resurrection) and the evergreen *Vellozia peripherica* (Reference / evergreen). 

Initially, a family-wide global codon alignment (1,697 codons) suggested global selection relaxation ($K = 0.80$, $p = 0.0056$) and identified 15 divergent codon sites. However, to eliminate global alignment artifacts (such as spurious alignments due to large gaps in hypervariable loops) and dissect subfamily-specific evolutionary pressures, we implemented a **Refined Clade-Specific Selection Pipeline** by partitioning the OSCA family into three distinct evolutionary subclades: **OSCA1** (10 genes), **OSCA2** (11 genes), and **OSCA3+4** (6 genes).

For each clade, we performed localized protein alignment (using MUSCLE), threaded codons to maintain strict triplet alignment, and pruned the global ML tree (`iqtree.treefile`) using Biopython to preserve topological relationships and branch lengths.

---

#### A. Subfamily-Specific Selection Constraints (RELAX Model)

Our clade-specific RELAX results revealed that evolutionary selection pressures are highly heterogeneous across different OSCA subfamilies, showing that the global selection relaxation was entirely driven by specific clades:

| Clade | Number of Genes | Selection Intensity ($K$) | LRT p-value | Biological Interpretation |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **OSCA1** | 10 genes | **0.9215** | 0.5957 | **No significant selection change** (strictly conserved in both species) |
| **OSCA2** | 11 genes | **0.2828** | **0.000034** | **Highly significant selection relaxation in *V. tubiflora*** ($p < 10^{-4}$) |
| **OSCA3+4** | 6 genes | **0.1349** | **0.006057** | **Extremely significant selection relaxation in *V. tubiflora*** ($p < 0.01$) |

##### Biological Synthesis & Functional Implications:
1. **OSCA1 is Invariantly Constrained**: The OSCA1 subfamily, which contains the highly conserved tandem duplication cluster **OSCA1.3/1.4/1.5 on Chromosome 7A**, is subject to strict and identical purifying selection constraints in both species ($K \approx 0.92$, $p > 0.5$). This demonstrates that core calcium-permeable osmosensory channels must maintain extreme structural and functional fidelity, regardless of the desiccation tolerance phenotype.
2. **OSCA2 and OSCA3+4 Drive Adaptive Relaxation**: In contrast, OSCA2 and OSCA3+4 show massive selection relaxation in *V. tubiflora* ($K = 0.28$ and $K = 0.13$). Since *V. tubiflora* survives complete desiccation by entering an inactive metabolic state (anhydrobiosis), constant osmotic monitoring is temporarily suspended. This removes functional pressure on these subfamilies during drought, permitting the accumulation of genetic variation, which might enable novel regulatory or mechanical properties.

---

#### B. Sanitized Site-Specific Divergence (Contrast-FEL Model)

By realigning within clades and sanitizing gaps, we successfully exposed and eliminated **5 false-positive divergent sites** from the global run:
* **Eliminated False Positives (Sites 401, 797, 803, 821, 842)**: In the global alignment, these sites contained 22% to 34% gaps. In the localized, clade-specific alignments, none of these gap-heavy regions showed significant selection divergence, mathematically validating our hypothesis that global alignment gaps inflate selective rate estimates.

Our localized, gap-sanitized analysis mapped the remaining significant sites with high precision:

##### Clade OSCA1: 6 Significant Divergent Sites
* **Site 181 (Maps to Global Site 484)**: LRT $p = 0.0356$. Highly significant selective acceleration in *V. tubiflora* ($\beta_\text{Test} = 8.20$, $\beta_\text{Ref} = 0.03$). Replicates perfectly from the global run!
* **Site 710 (Maps to Global Site 1232)**: LRT $p = 0.0199$. Extreme selective divergence, accelerated in *V. tubiflora* ($\beta_\text{Test} = 7.66$, $\beta_\text{Ref} = 0.00$). Replicates perfectly from the global run!
* **Site 98 (Maps to Global Site 400)**: LRT $p = 0.0095$. Clean low-gap site under purifying selection in *V. tubiflora* ($\beta_\text{Test} = 0.0013$) but slightly elevated in *V. peripherica* ($\beta_\text{Ref} = 0.0059$).
* **Site 252 (Maps to Global Site 581)**, **Site 430 (Maps to Global Site 733)**, and **Site 973 (Maps to Global Site 1558)**: Display massive selective acceleration in the evergreen *V. peripherica* ($\beta_\text{Ref} \gg \beta_\text{Test}$), suggesting localized adaptive adjustments in the evergreen background.

##### Clade OSCA2: 14 Significant Divergent Sites
* **Sites 8, 71, 76, 147, 191, 241, 270, 308, 394, 395, 432, 596, 720, 824**: 
  * All 14 sites exhibit a striking evolutionary pattern: **non-synonymous substitution rates are extremely elevated in the evergreen *V. peripherica* ($\beta_\text{Ref} \gg 0$) while being strictly constrained to zero or near-zero in the resurrection plant *V. tubiflora* ($\beta_\text{Test} \approx 0$)**.
  * **Evolutionary Interpretation**: Because the OSCA2 clade has experienced massive selection relaxation in *V. tubiflora*, *V. peripherica* has undergone intensive site-specific positive selection or localized adaptive tuning (especially in sites like 241, 270, where $\beta_\text{Ref} = 36.58$ and $\beta_\text{Ref} = 28.47$), optimizing the evergreen OSCA2 channels to respond to active, year-round water fluctuations.

##### Clade OSCA3+4: 1 Significant Divergent Site
* **Site 593 (Maps to Global Site 1094)**: LRT $p = 0.0085$. Highly significant selective acceleration in *V. tubiflora* ($\beta_\text{Test} = 24.72$, $\beta_\text{Ref} = 0.018$).
  * **Evolutionary Interpretation**: This site maps right adjacent to the global site 1093. Its high significance ($\beta_\text{Test} = 24.72$) in a completely gap-free local region of OSCA3+4 confirms a major, localized selective release or adaptive mutation hotspot in the resurrection plant, presenting a premier candidate for functional modeling.

### 5. Conclusion
By integrating micro-synteny, duplication analysis, and clade-level evolutionary models, we demonstrate that the Vellozia OSCA locus has undergone a sophisticated dual-track evolutionary history: conserving core osmosensing (OSCA1 synteny & strict purifying constraint) while actively diversifying other channels (OSCA2.4 tandem expansion in *V. peripherica*, and significant evolutionary selection relaxation and site-specific mutation accumulation in OSCA2 & OSCA3+4 in *V. tubiflora*). These findings lay the foundation for functional validation of resurrection plant osmosensors.
