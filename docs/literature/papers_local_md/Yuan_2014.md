Fang Yuan, Huimin Yang, Yan Xue, Dongdong Kong, Rui Ye,

Nature 514, 367–371 (2014); doi:10.1038/nature13593
While we were working on this Letter, Hou et al.1 reported the cloning
of AtCSC1, an osmosensitive calcium permeable cation channel. AtCSC1
is a close homologue of OSCA1, which we identified and characterized
via forward genetic screens. Whether AtCSC1 functions as an osmo-
sensitive channel in the plasma membrane or endomembranes in planta
as well as its physiological functions remains to be determined.
