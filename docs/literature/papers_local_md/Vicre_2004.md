# Insights into the cellular mechanisms of desiccation tolerance among angiosperm resurrection plant species

**Authors:** Maïté Vicré, Jill M. Farrant, Azeddine Driouich
**Journal:** *Plant, Cell & Environment*, 27(11): 1329–1340 (2004)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-3040.2004.01212.x

## Abstract
During desiccation, resurrection plants experience severe mechanical stress associated with cellular volume reduction. In eudicot resurrection plants such as Craterostigma plantagineum, primary cell walls possess remarkable plastic deformability, undergoing reversible, accordion-like folding (concertina-like folding) upon turgor loss. This prevents mechanical rupture of the plasma membrane from the cell wall, allowing the protoplast to contract harmoniously with the shrinking cell boundary.
