# Changes in leaf hexokinase activity and metabolite levels in response to drying in the desiccation-tolerant species Sporobolus stapfianus and Xerophyta viscosa

**Authors:** Allison Whittaker, A. Bochicchio, C. Vazzana, G. Lindsey, Jill M. Farrant
**Journal:** *Journal of Experimental Botany*, 52(358): 961–969 (2001)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jexbot/52.358.961

## Abstract
This study investigates the physiological and biochemical mechanisms that allow resurrection plants—specifically the grass Sporobolus stapfianus and the monocot Xerophyta viscosa—to survive extreme water loss. During dehydration, leaves of X. viscosa accumulate massive concentrations of sucrose and raffinose family oligosaccharides (RFOs) to stabilize membranes and promote vitrification of the cytoplasm, coordinated with regulated down-regulation of hexokinase activity to prevent substrate exhaustion and oxidative damage.
