# FastTree 2 - Approximately Maximum-Likelihood Trees for Large Alignments

**Authors:** Price, M. N., Dehal, P. S., & Arkin, A. P. (2010)
**Identifier/DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0009490

## Abstract

FastTree infers approximately-maximum-likelihood phylogenetic trees from alignments of nucleotide or amino acid sequences with high speed and scalability.
