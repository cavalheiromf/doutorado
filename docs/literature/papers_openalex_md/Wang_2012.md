# MCScanX: a toolkit for detection and evolutionary analysis of gene synteny and collinearity

**Authors:** Wang, Y., et al. (2012)
**Identifier/DOI:** 10.1093/nar/gks101

## Abstract

MCScanX provides algorithms for detecting syntenic and collinear blocks within and between genomes, and classifying gene duplication mechanisms into whole-genome, tandem, proximal, transposed, and dispersed duplicates.
