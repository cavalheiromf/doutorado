2.6. Stomata aperture and resistance bioassays

    Arabidopsis stomatal closure was measured as described previously
[22]. Rosette leaves were detached from 3–4-week-old plants and
ﬂoated in a solution containing 10 mM KCl, 2 mM CaCl2, and 10 mM
MES-Tris at pH 6.15 for 2 h in a growth chamber under light (120 μmol
m−2 s−1) at 22 °C. Sorbitol (200 mM) or NaCl (100 mM) was separately
added to the solutions followed by incubation for 2 h. Stomatal aper-
tures were imaged using a DP72 digital camera (Olympus Corp.) at-
tached to a ﬂuorescence microscope BX51 (Olympus Corp.) and ana-
lysed using DP2-BAW software (Olympus Corp.).

2.7. Osmotic stress-related physiological analysis

    After germination, Arabidopsis seedlings were transferred to half-
strength Murashige and Skoog medium containing 150 mM sorbitol or
125 mM NaCl for 10 days; then, the roots were photographed and root
length measured. For leaf water loss assays, fully expanded rosette
leaves were detached from 3-week-old seedlings and maintained under
40–50 % relative humidity and a constant temperature of 26 °C. Each
sample of 10 individual leaves was weighed at the indicated time, and
water loss was calculated with respect to the initial fresh weight.

2.8. Immunoblot assay

    The protein level of OsOSCA1.4-HA*3 in transgenic Arabidopsis
plants was monitored with Western blot according to a prior study with
modiﬁcation [25]. The total proteins were extracted from three-week-
old Arabidopsis leaves in buﬀer (62.5 mM Tris-HCl [pH 6.8], 2% sodium
dodecyl sulphate [SDS], 10 % glycerine, and 1 mM β-mercaptoethanol)
and the protein concentration was determined using a Bio-Rad Protein
Assay Kit with bovine serum albumin as the standard. Next, the proteins
were resolved by SDS-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis and trans-
ferred to a polyvinylidene diﬂuoride membrane (Millipore, USA) under
200 mA constant current for 45 min in transfer buﬀer (12.5 mM Tris-
HCl, 192 mM glycine, and 10 % methanol; pH 8.3). Anti-HA (Abcam,
1:10,000) and anti-Actin (Sigma-Aldrich, 1:1000) antibodies were used
to visualise protein bands.

2.9. Statistical analysis

   Statistical analyses were carried out using the Data Processing
System [26]. We assumed that the data are normally distributed, then
one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) and Tukey multiple-range test
were conducted to assess the signiﬁcance of diﬀerences. NS indicates as
no signiﬁcance diﬀerence; * indicates signiﬁcance diﬀerence
(P < 0.05) and ** indicates extreme signiﬁcance diﬀerence
(P < 0.01).
