# Spermidine-mediated recovery of the resurrection plant Xerophyta viscosa from dehydration: characterization of a galactinol synthase gene

**Authors:** Shaun Peters, Sagadevan G. Mundree, Jill M. Farrant, Felix Keller
**Journal:** *Journal of Experimental Botany*, 58(13): 3775–3787 (2007)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erm230

## Abstract
Galactinol synthase (GolS) catalyzes the first committed step in the biosynthesis of raffinose family oligosaccharides (RFOs), transferring a galactosyl moiety from UDP-galactose to myo-inositol. In the resurrection plant Xerophyta viscosa, GolS expression and enzyme activity increase markedly during desiccation, leading to substantial accumulation of raffinose and stachyose. These oligosaccharides protect cell membranes and prevent sucrose crystallization during cytoplasmic vitrification.
