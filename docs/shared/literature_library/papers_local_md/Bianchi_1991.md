# Novel carbohydrate metabolism in the resurrection plant Craterostigma plantagineum

**Authors:** Giorgio Bianchi, A. Gamba, C. Murelli, Francesco Salamini, Dorothea Bartels
**Journal:** *The Plant Journal*, 1(3): 355–359 (1991)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-313x.1991.00355.x

## Abstract
In the fully hydrated state, leaves of the resurrection plant Craterostigma plantagineum accumulate high concentrations of an unusual 8-carbon monosaccharide, 2-octulose. During drying, 2-octulose is rapidly converted into sucrose, reaching up to 40% of the dry weight, which acts as a major osmoprotectant and vitrifying agent in dry tissues.
