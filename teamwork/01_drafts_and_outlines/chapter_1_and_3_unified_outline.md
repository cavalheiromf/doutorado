# Macroevolution and Genomic Divergence in Velloziaceae: from Deep Time to Contrasting Survival Strategies

## 1. Introduction (The Funnel Approach)
*   **The Macro Panorama:** Overview of the *campos rupestres* and the diversity of Velloziaceae. The challenge of desiccation and the Gondwana hypothesis (when and how did desiccation tolerance evolve?).
*   **The Micro Focus:** Desiccation tolerance is not universal in the family. Introduce *V. tubiflora* (resurrection/desiccation-tolerant) and *V. peripherica* (evergreen/desiccation-sensitive) as a sister-species pair with divergent physiological and ecological niches.
*   **Objectives:** 
    1. Resolve the phylogeny and divergence time of the family.
    2. Assess natural selection pressures across the clade.
    3. Investigate which gene families expanded/contracted during the divergence of the ecological strategies of the two focal *Vellozia* species.

## 2. Methods (The Integrated Pipeline)
1.  **Orthology (OrthoFinder):** The 7-taxon dataset, protein sets, and orthogroup inference.
2.  **Phylogenomics and Dating (IQ-TREE + MCMCtree):** Concatenation, species tree inference, and fossil-calibrated molecular dating.
3.  **Gene Family Evolution (CAFE5):** Mapping family size changes using the *new dated tree*.
4.  **Positive Selection (HyPhy):** Identification of genes under positive selection (e.g., aBSREL / BUSTED).
5.  **Functional Annotation and Enrichment:** Focused specifically on the orthogroups expanding/contracting between *V. tubiflora* and *V. peripherica*.

## 3. Results
*   **PART 1: The Family Panorama (Macroevolution):**
    *   The robust phylogenomic tree (concordance factors and bootstrap).
    *   Divergence ages (Node 13, etc.) — *Addresses whether the Gondwanan hypothesis holds or if the origin is more recent.*
    *   Global patterns of natural selection in the family (HyPhy results).
*   **PART 2: The Focal Divergence (*V. tubiflora* vs *V. peripherica*):**
    *   Differences in orthogroup architecture (species-specific gene counts).
    *   Asymmetry of expansion/contraction (updated CAFE5 results): Who gained and who lost genes?
    *   Functions explaining the ecological divergence: Enrichment of chloroplast-related genes in *V. tubiflora* vs. receptor kinases and metabolic pathways in *V. peripherica*.

## 4. Discussion (The Synthesis)
*   How the dating changes (or reaffirms) what is known about Velloziaceae biogeography.
*   What genomic signatures (CAFE5 and HyPhy) tell us about the cost of being "evergreen" vs. "resurrection".
*   Desiccation tolerance is not a simple on/off switch, but involves the turnover of multiple gene families.
