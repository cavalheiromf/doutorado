### Figure 1. Bayesian molecular dating chronogram and historical biogeography of Velloziaceae.

**Figure 1. Deep-time phylogenomic chronogram and historical biogeography resolve the Oligocene trans-Atlantic disjunction and Late Miocene radiation in Velloziaceae.**

**(A) Bayesian molecular dating chronogram across seven annotated angiosperm genomes.** Divergence times and 95% Highest Posterior Density (HPD) credibility intervals were estimated using the approximate likelihood MCMCtree algorithm in PAML v4.10.8 under an independent rates log-normal relaxed molecular clock model (`clock = 2`), based on a concatenated supermatrix of 1,513 strictly single-copy 1:1 orthologs spanning 732,066 aligned amino acid sites with 0% missing data. Branch topology was inferred via partitioned maximum likelihood in IQ-TREE v2.3.6 under best-fit ModelFinder amino acid substitution models, yielding unanimous 100% Ultrafast Bootstrap (UFBoot2) support across all internal bifurcations (indicated by solid dark circles at nodes). Horizontal semi-transparent rectangular bars behind internal nodes delineate the 95% HPD posterior age credibility intervals. Five paleontologically vetted fossil calibrations are indicated by anchor badges with specified uniform prior ranges: 
1. Root node (**t_n8**, *Arabidopsis thaliana* vs. Monocots): mean posterior age = **138.48 Ma** (95% HPD: 130.30–145.25 Ma; uniform prior: 130.0–145.0 Ma, Early Cretaceous, Valanginian–Hauterivian);
2. Monocot crown node (**t_n9**, *Dioscorea alata* vs. Pandanales): mean posterior age = **121.90 Ma** (95% HPD: 115.19–128.19 Ma; uniform prior: 115.0–128.0 Ma, Early Cretaceous, Aptian);
3. Pandanales crown node (**t_n10**, *Pandanus tectorius* vs. Velloziaceae): mean posterior age = **101.26 Ma** (95% HPD: 95.19–105.68 Ma; uniform prior: 90.0–105.0 Ma, mid-Cretaceous, Cenomanian);
4. Velloziaceae crown node (**t_n11**, *Acanthochlamys bracteata* vs. Core Velloziaceae): mean posterior age = **78.21 Ma** (95% HPD: 73.90–86.34 Ma; uniform prior: 75.0–90.0 Ma, Late Cretaceous, Campanian);
5. *Vellozia* sister divergence node (**t_n13**, *Vellozia peripherica* vs. *Vellozia tubiflora*): mean posterior age = **9.74 Ma** (95% HPD: 5.29–15.03 Ma; 95% CI: 5.71–15.91 Ma; uniform prior: 2.0–25.0 Ma, Late Miocene, Tortonian).

Crucially, the intercontinental node uniting the African resurrection lineage (*Xerophyta viscosa*) and South American *Vellozia* (**t_n12**, Core Velloziaceae) was left completely **unconstrained** in the prior specification to provide an unbiased empirical test of biogeographical hypotheses; MCMCtree estimated the mean posterior divergence age at **31.92 Ma** (95% HPD: 19.26–43.66 Ma; 95% CI: 22.47–50.65 Ma, Early Oligocene, Rupelian), highlighted by a prominent amber callout badge. Clade background fills and terminal tip icons designate distinct geographical provenances and ecophysiological syndromes: Eudicot root outgroup (*A. thaliana*, grey), Monocot outgroup (*D. alata*, sky blue), Pandanaceae sister family (*P. tectorius*, sky blue), Chinese subalpine Hengduan scree endemic (*A. bracteata*, purple), African inselberg resurrection specialist (*X. viscosa*, amber), South American *campos rupestres* evergreen generalist (*V. peripherica*, emerald green), and South American *campos rupestres* resurrection saxicole (*V. tubiflora*, golden amber). The lower horizontal banner displays the chronostratigraphic geological timescale adhering to the International Commission on Stratigraphy (ICS) standard colors, partitioned into Periods (Cretaceous, Paleogene, Neogene, Quaternary) and Epochs (Early Cretaceous, Late Cretaceous, Paleocene, Eocene, Oligocene, Miocene, Pliocene, Pleistocene, Holocene).

**(B) Paleogeographical and macroevolutionary hypothesis testing framework.** Infographic synthesis comparing competing biogeographical scenarios across three analytical cards:
- **Hypothesis 1 (Refuted) — Strict Cretaceous Gondwanan Vicariance:** Postulated that the African–South American disjunction arose via tectonic rifting and the opening of the South Atlantic Ocean (~100–120 Ma, Aptian–Albian). The empirical molecular dating of 31.92 Ma post-dates the tectonic separation by more than 68 million years, with the upper 95% HPD limit (43.66 Ma) falling tens of millions of years after continental connections were severed, decisively refuting strict vicariance.
- **Hypothesis 2 (Supported) — Mid-Cenozoic Westward Trans-Atlantic Long-Distance Dispersal (LDD):** Demonstrates that ancestral Core Velloziaceae crossed the Atlantic Ocean during the Early Oligocene (~31.92 Ma) following the Eocene–Oligocene Transition (EOT, ~33.9 Ma). Global cooling and Antarctic glaciation induced major sea-level lowstands, exposing subaerial volcanic archipelagos along the Walvis Ridge and Rio Grande Rise that served as island stepping stones. Trans-oceanic transit was facilitated by intensified easterly trade winds, equatorial surface currents, and the extreme physical resilience of desiccation-tolerant vegetative fragments and anhydrobiotic seeds.
- **Macroevolutionary Timeline Synthesis:** Chronological milestone axis summarizing the deep-time evolutionary trajectory: Angiosperm Root (138.48 Ma) $\rightarrow$ Monocot Radiation (121.90 Ma) $\rightarrow$ Pandanales Split (101.26 Ma) $\rightarrow$ Crown Velloziaceae (78.21 Ma) $\rightarrow$ Trans-Atlantic LDD (31.92 Ma) $\rightarrow$ Sister Speciation in the Espinhaço Range *campos rupestres* (9.74 Ma).

### Figure 3. Gene family turnover dynamics across Pandanales and asymmetric functional expansions between resurrection and evergreen *Vellozia*.

**Figure 3. Stochastic birth-and-death modeling in CAFE5 reveals ancestral establishment of anhydrobiosis machinery and asymmetric gene family expansions supporting the evergreen habit in *Vellozia peripherica*.**

**(A) Dated species tree annotated with lineage-specific gene family expansions and contractions.** Global gene family turnover was modeled across 12,276 curated orthogroups using CAFE v5.1.0 under an error-corrected two-rate category Gamma distribution model ($k=2$, $\epsilon = 0.006092$). The model demonstrated optimal convergence across independent replicates ($-\ln L = 107,721.36$), estimating a global turnover rate of $\lambda = 0.003637 \pm 0.0000004$ duplications/losses per gene per million years ($\alpha = 0.89715 \pm 0.00008$). Numbers on each branch indicate gene family gains (+ green badge) and losses (- red badge) based on a high-stringency Tier&nbsp;A consensus of 188 families that passed genome-wide False Discovery Rate correction (FDR $q \le 0.05$) across all independent runs. Key macroevolutionary transitions include:
- **Crown Core Velloziaceae MRCA (Node 12, 31.92 Ma):** Synchronized expansion of **100 gene families** alongside 17 contractions (net positive balance: **+83 families**), establishing the conserved homologous anhydrobiotic toolkit (Late Embryogenesis Abundant [LEA] proteins, small heat shock proteins [sHSPs], raffinose family oligosaccharide [RFO] synthases, and sucrose synthases) prior to the trans-Atlantic disjunction.
- **Asymmetric Divergence in *Vellozia* Sister Species (Node 13, 9.74 Ma):** 
  * *Vellozia tubiflora* (Resurrection specialist): 31 significant family expansions and 80 contractions (net balance: **-49 families**), reflecting a streamlined, dormancy-optimized genome supported by 15.96% local duplicates.
  * *Vellozia peripherica* (Evergreen generalist): **72 significant family expansions** and 61 contractions (net balance: **+11 families**), directly coupled to an excess of +1,547 local tandem and proximal gene duplicates (accounting for 21.25% of its gene catalog), substantiating "The Genomic Burden of Being Evergreen."

**(B) Comparative functional enrichment of lineage-specific gene family expansions.** Horizontal divergent barplots contrasting the top expanded functional categories between *V. tubiflora* (golden amber bars `#E69F00`, dormancy-competent proteome preservation) and *V. peripherica* (emerald green bars `#009E73`, environmental perception, continuous proteostasis, and structural defense):
- **Expansions in *V. peripherica* (Evergreen):**
  1. *F-box & E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Proteasome Degradation Complex:* +51 paralogs in `OG0000026` (F-box/LRR and SKP1-Cullin-F-box [SCF] adaptors), driving selective 26S proteasomal clearance of photo-oxidatively damaged proteins in permanently active leaves.
  2. *Leucine-Rich Repeat Receptor-Like Kinases (LRR-RLKs):* Multiple expanded arrays functioning as cell surface sensors monitoring cell wall-plasma membrane mechanical integrity and turgor pressure.
  3. *NBS-LRR (NLR) Disease Resistance Sentinels:* Expanded arrays safeguarding hydrated foliar tissues against opportunistic fungal and bacterial pathogens.
  4. *Cytochrome P450 Monooxygenases & UDP-glucosyltransferases:* `OG0000281` expanded to 14 copies via local tandem arrays on chromosomes 1A and 4A, catalyzing biosynthesis of cuticular waxes, suberin barriers, and protective flavonoid antioxidants.
  5. *Anchored Chloroplastic NADH Dehydrogenase (NDH) Module:* Coordinated chromosomal expansion of 42 anchored copies across chromosomes 1A, 2A, 4A, and 7A (`OG0000014`, `OG0000156`, `OG0000278`), driving cyclic electron flow (CEF) around Photosystem I to dissipate excess excitation energy. Apparent NDH expansions in *V. tubiflora* were confirmed as assembly fragmentation artifacts on unanchored short contigs (`ctg230`–`ctg415`).

**(C) Diagnostic gene family evolutionary trajectories & conceptual synthesis.** Slope charts tracking exact copy number transitions from the ancestral *Vellozia* MRCA to *Vtub* and *Vper* across diagnostic orthogroups, accompanied by an executive synthesis card summarizing the ecological trade-off between dormancy-mediated protection and the heavy genomic investment required to sustain active foliage under chronic montane stress.

### Figure 5. Macroevolutionary synthesis of contrasting survival strategies in Neotropical *campos rupestres*: the physiological and genomic trade-offs of vegetative desiccation tolerance versus evergreen persistence.

**Figure 5. Conceptual macroevolutionary model illustrating the physiological, biochemical, and genomic divergence between resurrection (*Vellozia tubiflora*) and evergreen (*Vellozia peripherica*) survival strategies under Old, Climatically-Buffered, Infertile Landscapes (OCBIL) theory.**

**(A) Shared macroevolutionary matrix in the Espinhaço Range *campos rupestres*.** Both species share an ancient common ancestry shaped by the Oligocene trans-Atlantic dispersal (31.92 Ma), an ancestral expansion of 218 desiccation-protective gene families (+528 net genes) at the Core Velloziaceae MRCA (Node 9), and Late Miocene sister speciation (9.74 Ma) operating within an identical chromosomal framework ($2n = 18$, $x = 9$, 297 collinear syntenic blocks, 0 translocations). Both taxa inhabit severe abiotic environments characterized by intense solar UV-B radiation, hyper-impoverished quartzite sands (bioavailable phosphorus $< 1\text{ mg/kg}$), and 5–6 months of acute seasonal water deficit.

**(B) Ancestral poikilochlorophyllous resurrection strategy in *Vellozia tubiflora* (Saxicolous Specialist).** Occupying exposed, soil-less quartzite and granitic rock surfaces subjected to extreme water potential drops ($\Psi < -100\text{ MPa}$):
- *Dry-Season Physiology (Anhydrobiosis):* Slow, controlled dismantling of Photosystem II and thylakoid grana into non-photosynthetic desiccoplasts; complete chlorophyll degradation and protective accumulation of vacuolar anthocyanins and non-reducing disaccharides (sucrose, raffinose, stachyose); cytoplasmic vitrification into an amorphous biological glass, halting all cellular metabolism and achieving near-zero mortality under desiccating conditions.
- *Genomic & Molecular Adaptations:* Streamlined gene turnover (net -101 genes) with constitutive priming of stress regulons; lineage-specific expansions in plastid protein import (+55 TOC/TIC translocons in `OG0000016`, +22 in `OG0000087`), chloroplastic NDH complex (+29 in `OG0000036`, +24 in `OG0000048`), Photosystem II core protection (+24 in `OG0000069`), and lipid synthesis (+23 ACC in `OG0000072`); intense positive selection on 17 PLS-class PPR editing factors ($\omega$ up to 4.91), catalytic DYW deaminase (`OG0011950`, $\omega = 3.80$), organellar RRM (`OG0012126`, $\omega = 4.50$), and DnaJ/Hsp40 chaperones (`OG0011532`, $\omega = 3.86$).
- *Rehydration Kinetics:* Rapid structural and functional reactivation (24–72 h), relying on stored, pre-edited organellar transcripts to translate essential respiratory and photosynthetic proteins without requiring de novo transcription.

**(C) Derived secondary evergreen strategy in *Vellozia peripherica* (Fissure/Crevice Generalist).** Occupying deeper rock fissures and micro-depressions with soil accumulation and moderate water potentials ($\Psi \approx -1.5\text{ to }-2.0\text{ MPa}$):
- *Dry-Season Physiology (Continuous Buffering):* Avoids desiccation by maintaining fully hydrated, green, photosynthetically active foliage throughout the year via sensitive stomatal regulation, deep root access, and internal stem water storage. However, continuous metabolic activity under high vapor pressure deficits generates chronic photo-oxidative stress, requiring persistent antioxidant defenses and exposing permanent foliage to foliar pathogens.
- *\"The Genomic Burden of Being Evergreen\":* Asymmetric gene family expansion comprising **341 significant expanding families** (net gain: **+723 genes**; ~3.7-fold higher than *Vtub*), heavily enriched in:
  1. *F-box & E3 Ubiquitin Ligases (+51 paralogs in OG0000026):* Driving continuous 26S proteasome-mediated degradation of photo-oxidatively damaged proteins;
  2. *LRR-RLK Receptor Kinases (+57/+29/+25 paralogs):* Constantly monitoring cell wall-plasma membrane mechanical strain under fluctuating turgor;
  3. *NBS-LRR Disease Resistance Sentinels (+22/+17 paralogs):* Protecting nutrient-rich green foliage against opportunistic foliar pathogens;
  4. *Cytochrome P450 Monooxygenases (+88 genes):* Synthesizing protective cuticular wax barriers and secondary antioxidant flavonoids.
- *Ecological Vulnerability:* Susceptible to catastrophic xylem cavitation, hydraulic failure, and irreversible tissue death if leaf relative water content drops below ~30% during anomalous, multi-year mega-droughts.

**(D) Central evolutionary trade-off balance scale.** Contrast along the 9.74 Ma divergence axis between *Quiescent Resilience (Dormancy)*—which secures near-zero mortality on bare rock at the expense of low annual carbon gain and slow growth—versus *Competitive Productivity (Continuous Buffering)*—which maximizes photosynthetic assimilation during favorable periods but requires an extensive, high-maintenance genomic infrastructure and incurs fatal cavitation vulnerability under extreme climatic anomalies.

**(E) Synoptic comparative synthesis matrix.** Structured four-row comparative matrix summarizing Microhabitat & Water Potential ($\Psi$), Dry-Season Physiology & Cellular State, Genomic Investment & Lineage Expansions, and Primary Evolutionary Trade-offs between *V. tubiflora* and *V. peripherica*.

### Figura 5 – Síntese macroevolutiva de estratégias contrastantes de sobrevivência nos campos rupestres neotropicais: custos fisiológicos e genômicos da dessecação vegetativa versus hábito perene.

**Figura 5. Modelo conceitual macroevolutivo ilustrando a divergência fisiológica, bioquímica e genômica entre as estratégias de ressurreição (*Vellozia tubiflora*) e hábito perene (*Vellozia peripherica*) sob a teoria de Paisagens Antigas, Climaticamente Tamponadas e Infértilizadas (OCBIL).**

**(A) Matriz macroevolutiva compartilhada nos campos rupestres da Cadeia do Espinhaço.** Ambas as espécies compartilham uma ancestralidade comum profunda marcada pela dispersão transatlântica no Oligoceno (31,92 Ma), uma expansão ancestral de 218 famílias gênicas protetoras contra dessecação (+528 genes líquidos) no ancestral de Velloziaceae Nuclear (Nó 9) e especiação no Mioceno Superior (9,74 Ma), compartilhando a mesma arquitetura cromossômica estável ($2n = 18$, $x = 9$, 297 blocos sintênicos colineares, 0 translocações). Ambas habitam ambientes de severo estresse abiótico com intensa radiação solar UV-B, solos quartzíticos hiperempobrecidos (fósforo disponível $< 1\text{ mg/kg}$) e 5 a 6 meses de seca sazonal severa.

**(B) Estratégia ancestral de ressurreição poiquiloclorófila em *Vellozia tubiflora* (Especialista Saxícola).** Ocupando superfícies de rochas quartzíticas e graníticas expostas sem acúmulo de solo, sob potenciais hídricos extremos ($\Psi < -100\text{ MPa}$):
- *Fisiologia na Estação Seca (Anidrobiose):* Desmantelamento controlado do Fotossistema II e dos tilacoides em desiccoplastos não fotossintetizantes; degradação total de clorofilas e acúmulo protetor de antocianinas vacuolares e dissacarídeos não redutores (sacarose, rafinose, estaquiose); vitrificação citoplasmática em vidro biológico amorfo, paralisando o metabolismo celular e conferindo mortalidade próxima de zero na dessecação.
- *Adaptações Genômicas e Moleculares:* Rotatividade genômica enxuta (saldo de -101 genes) com regulons de estresse constitutivamente pré-ativados; expansões linhagem-específicas no transporte de proteínas plastidiais (+55 translocons TOC/TIC em `OG0000016`, +22 em `OG0000087`), no complexo NDH (+29 em `OG0000036`, +24 em `OG0000048`), na proteção do PSII (+24 em `OG0000069`) e na síntese lipídica (+23 ACC em `OG0000072`); forte seleção positiva em 17 fatores de edição PPR da classe PLS ($\omega$ até 4,91), desaminase DYW (`OG0011950`, $\omega = 3,80$), RRM organelar (`OG0012126`, $\omega = 4,50$) e chaperonas DnaJ/Hsp40 (`OG0011532`, $\omega = 3,86$).
- *Cinética de Reidratação:* Reativação funcional e estrutural rápida (24–72 h), dependente de transcritos organelares pré-editados estocados para tradução de proteínas essenciais de respiração e fotossíntese sem necessidade de transcrição de novo.

**(C) Estratégia secundária derivada de hábito perene em *Vellozia peripherica* (Generalista de Fendas).** Ocupando fendas de rochas e microdepressões com acúmulo de solo e potenciais hídricos moderados ($\Psi \approx -1,5\text{ a }-2,0\text{ MPa}$):
- *Fisiologia na Estação Seca (Tamponamento Contínuo):* Evita a dessecação mantendo folhas totalmente hidratadas, verdes e fotossinteticamente ativas o ano todo por meio de controle estomático sensível, raízes profundas e armazenamento de água no pseudocaule. Contudo, a manutenção metabólica sob alto déficit de pressão de vapor impõe estresse foto-oxidativo crônico, exigindo síntese contínua de antioxidantes e expondo a copa permanente a patógenos foliares.
- *\"O Fardo Genômico do Hábito Perene\":* Expansão assimétrica maciça de **341 famílias gênicas significativas** (ganho líquido: **+723 genes**; ~3,7 vezes superior a *Vtub*), enriquecidas em:
  1. *Ubiquitina Ligases E3 e F-box (+51 parálogos em OG0000026):* Promovendo a depuração proteassômica 26S contínua de proteínas danificadas por estresse foto-oxidativo;
  2. *Quinases Receptórias LRR-RLK (+57/+29/+25 parálogos):* Monitorando continuamente a deformação mecânica entre parede celular e membrana plasmática sob flutuações de turgor;
  3. *Receptores Imunológicos de Resistência NBS-LRR (+22/+17 parálogos):* Protegendo folhas verdes ricas em nutrientes contra patógenos oportunistas;
  4. *Monooxigenases do Citocromo P450 (+88 genes):* Sintetizando ceras cuticulares espessas e flavonoides antioxidantes.
- *Vulnerabilidade Ecológica:* Suscetibilidade à cavitação xilemática catastrófica, falha hidráulica e morte tecidual irreversível caso o conteúdo relativo de água foliar caia abaixo de ~30% durante megassecas anômalas prolongadas.

**(D) Balança comparativa de compromissos evolutivos (*trade-offs*).** Contraste ao longo do eixo de divergência de 9,74 Ma entre *Resiliência Quiescente (Dormência)*—que assegura sobrevivência quase total em rochas nuas ao custo de menor ganho de carbono anual e crescimento lento—versus *Produtividade Competitiva (Tamponamento Contínuo)*—que maximiza o ganho fotossintético em períodos favoráveis mas impõe uma infraestrutura genômica pesada e cara de manutenção, além de vulnerabilidade fatal à cavitação em secas extremas.

**(E) Matriz comparativa de síntese sinóptica.** Quadro comparativo estruturado em quatro linhas confrontando Micro-habitat e Potencial Hídrico ($\Psi$), Fisiologia na Estação Seca e Estado Celular, Investimento Genômico e Expansões de Linhagem, e Principais Compromissos Evolutivos (*Trade-offs*) entre *V. tubiflora* e *V. peripherica*.
